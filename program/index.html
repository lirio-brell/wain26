Keynote Speaker: Dr. Zijian Zhang, Jilin University

Title: From Semantic Understanding to Recommendation Decisions: The Role of Large Language Models

Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) provide rich semantic information about items and user preferences. How can recommender systems combine this information with behavioral signals to decide what to recommend? This talk begins with a brief overview of how LLMs enhance recommender systems, then examines three ways in which semantics can inform recommendation decisions. First, the talk explores how shared semantics enable knowledge transfer across domains and how this approach can extend to collaboration between autonomous recommender systems. It then examines how semantic and collaborative signals can be modeled separately and combined at the prediction stage to improve sequential recommendation. Finally, it considers how generative recommenders can encode these two types of information into distinct item identifiers and adapt their relative contributions to the user's interaction context. The talk concludes with a brief outlook on using LLMs to simulate user feedback for training and evaluating recommendation policies.

Biography

Dr. Zijian Zhang is an Assistant Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at Jilin University and an Honorary Visiting Scholar at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working closely with Prof. Irwin King. He received his doctoral degrees from Jilin University and City University of Hong Kong in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Hongwei Zhao and Prof. Xiangyu Zhao. His research interests include time series analysis, recommender systems, and large language models. Dr. Zhang has published more than 40 papers in international conferences and journals, including 15 as first or corresponding author. His work has received more than 1,700 citations, and his h-index is 19. He has presented tutorials on LLM-enhanced recommender systems and human mobility analysis at WWW 2025 and 2026, KDD 2025, and SSTD 2025. He has served on the (senior) program committees of conferences including NeurIPS, ICLR, KDD, WWW, and SIGIR, and has reviewed for journals including AIJ, TKDE, TCYB, TNNLS, and TOIS. He has also served as a session chair at WWW 2026, AAAI 2023, and CIKM 2023.
